Privacy Policy

Last updated: this version of the site.

What we collect

Symptoms IQ has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, date of birth, or any health record. The symptoms you browse are not transmitted to us.

Stored on your own device

Your saved guides, personal notes, language choice, and display settings (dark mode, text size, font) are stored in your own browser so the site remembers them on your next visit. They stay on your device and are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Feedback form

If you submit the feedback form, we receive what you typed: your rating, category, details, and your email address only if you chose to provide one. Submissions are handled by Netlify Forms, our hosting provider. We use them to fix problems and improve the site, and we do not sell or share them.

External lookups

The optional medical-term lookup in the Sources section sends the words you type to the U.S. National Library of Medicine's public Clinical Tables service so it can return matching official terms. That request goes directly from your browser to them, and is subject to their privacy practices, not ours.

Hosting

This site is hosted by Netlify, which may record standard server access logs (such as IP address and browser type) as part of delivering the page.

Children

This site is written for adults looking up everyday symptoms, including on behalf of a child. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the feedback form.
